How It Works

This page demonstrates how Brainwind's semantic annotations can trigger HTMX requests to fetch contextual information about entities. Click on any highlighted entity to load its details.

Sample Content: Coffee Supply Chain

The journey of Arabica coffee beans begins in the highlands of Ethiopia , where they are cultivated by local farming cooperatives .

The beans are processed using the washed process , which creates a clean, bright flavor profile . This method was refined during the 19th century .

🔍 Behind the Scenes

Each interactive node uses HTMX attributes:

<span 
  class="bw-node bw-interactive" 
  data-bw-type="Product" 
  data-bw-id="arabica-coffee"
  hx-get="#entity-arabica-coffee"
  hx-trigger="click"
  hx-target="#info-panel">
    Arabica coffee beans
</span>

In a real application, hx-get would point to an API endpoint that fetches entity data from your knowledge graph.
